# Brand - Aftergraph Cron Fabric

Product name: **Aftergraph Cron Fabric**.
Masterbrand: Aftergraph - infrastructure for verified intelligent systems.
This repo is infrastructure: it watches systems and reports evidence; it
does not execute, govern, or verify on behalf of other repos.

## Evidence boundary

Shared visual identity does not grant shared execution claims. A Cron
Fabric alert never inherits authority from runtime, execution,
conformance, or research repos. Every notify+ carries typed evidence.
